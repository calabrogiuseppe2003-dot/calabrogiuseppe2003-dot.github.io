About

I am a PhD student at ETH Zurich. I work in prof. David Kammer's Solid Mechanics research group. My interests lie in the development of computational methods for understanding and predicting the behaviour of solid materials and structures.

I am particularly interested in the interaction between computational solid mechanics and machine learning, with an emphasis on interpretable and physically meaningful models.

Research interests

My broader interests are in computational solid mechanics and data-driven modelling. In particular, I am interested in:

  • Scientific machine learning for solid mechanics;
  • Graph neural networks for the modelling and analysis of lattice metamaterials;
  • Topology optimization and inverse design of structures and fluid flows;
  • Finite element analysis and numerical methods for computational solid mechanics;
  • Preconditioning techniques and efficient iterative solvers for large-scale finite element problems.

Background

Before starting my PhD, I completed an MSc in Mathematical Modelling and Scientific Computing at the University of Oxford (2025–2026), graduating with Distinction. My master's work focused on large-scale topology optimisation for incompressible Navier–Stokes flows, with an emphasis on finite element methods and iterative solvers.

I previously obtained a BSc in Aerospace Engineering from Politecnico di Milano (2022–2025), graduating cum laude.
